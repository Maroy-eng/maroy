Everyday Life As a Student

by Valena,Jhamar C.

My name is Jhamar, and I am 15 years old. At this stage of my life, I am still discovering who I am and what I want to become in the future. I have many interests and hobbies that make my everyday life enjoyable. I like learning new things, creating things with my own hands, and challenging myself to become better.

One of the things I enjoy the most is making and building things. I like the feeling of starting with an idea and turning it into something real. I also enjoy drawing because it allows me to express my creativity and imagination. Whether I am building something or drawing, I always enjoy the process of creating something from my own ideas.

I also love learning, especially when it comes to math, science, and computer coding. Math is one of my favorite subjects because I enjoy solving problems and finding the correct answer. Science interests me because it helps me understand how the world works. I am also interested in coding because I can use it to create programs, games, and other things using a computer.

Gaming is another big part of my life. I enjoy playing different kinds of games, especially when I can challenge myself or compete with other people. Being competitive motivates me to improve my skills and try harder whenever I lose. Although I play games for fun, I also like the challenge of becoming better and reaching my goals.

Overall, I would describe myself as a creative, curious, and competitive person. I enjoy creating things, drawing, gaming, and learning about subjects that interest me. Since I am only 15, I know I still have a lot to learn and experience. I am excited to discover more about myself and hopefully use my interests and skills to build a successful future.
